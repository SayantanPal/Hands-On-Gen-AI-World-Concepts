NVIDIA reported record quarterly revenue of $46.74 billion for Q2 FY2026, representing approximately 56% year-over-year growth, while GAAP diluted EPS increased 61% to $1.08. 
Data Center demand remained the primary growth driver as hyperscalers and enterprise customers continued investing heavily in AI infrastructure. 
Management highlighted strong adoption of its Blackwell GPU platform, expansion of networking solutions, and growing demand for AI training and inference workloads. 
GAAP gross margin was 72.4%, reflecting product mix and inventory-related factors, while operating expenses increased as the company invested in infrastructure, engineering, and product development. 
For Q3 FY2026, management guided for revenue of approximately $54 billion, plus or minus 2%, and non-GAAP gross margin of approximately 73.5%. 
The company remains optimistic about long-term AI infrastructure spending and expects continued demand for its next-generation computing platforms. 
However, geopolitical tensions and US export restrictions affecting China shipments remain significant risks, alongside supply-chain capacity, customer concentration, and the possibility of elevated AI infrastructure spending moderating. 
The outlook remains strongly positive, although execution and regulatory developments will be important to sustaining growth.
