Apple reported a strong fourth quarter for fiscal 2025, generating revenue of $98700000000, representing 8% year-over-year growth, while adjusted diluted EPS came in at $1.85, up 13%. 
Services revenue reached a record $28.8 billion, growing 15%, while iPhone revenue increased 6% to approximately $49 billion. 
Mac revenue grew 13% to $8.7 billion, demonstrating healthy demand across the product portfolio. 
Management highlighted strong customer engagement, a record installed base, continued Services expansion, and increasing investment in AI capabilities and infrastructure. 
Gross margin reached 47.2%, supported by favorable product mix. 
For the December quarter, management guided for revenue growth of 10%–12%, with gross margin expected between 47% and 48%. Key risks include tariff-related costs, geopolitical uncertainty, rising research and development expenses, and the challenge of translating AI investments into meaningful monetization. 
Overall, management expressed confidence in continued product demand and Services growth heading into the holiday quarter.
