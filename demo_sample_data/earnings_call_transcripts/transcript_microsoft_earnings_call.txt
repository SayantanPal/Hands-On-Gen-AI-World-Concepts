Microsoft delivered a strong finish to fiscal 2025, reporting quarterly revenue of $76.4 billion, up 18% year-over-year, with diluted EPS of $3.65, increasing 24%. 
Microsoft Cloud revenue reached $46.7 billion, growing 27%, while Azure and other cloud services revenue increased 39%, driven by sustained enterprise demand and AI workloads. 
Management highlighted the growing adoption of Microsoft 365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot, Azure AI services, and enterprise cloud infrastructure. 
Commercial bookings exceeded $100 billion, reflecting strong customer commitments and long-term contracts. 
However, AI infrastructure expansion is creating significant capital expenditure requirements, with Q1 FY2026 capital expenditure expected to exceed $30 billion. 
Management expects another year of double-digit revenue and operating income growth in FY2026, while operating margins are expected to remain relatively unchanged. 
Azure revenue growth is expected to be approximately 37% in constant currency in the coming quarter, although capacity constraints may continue through the first half of the fiscal year. 
Key risks include infrastructure costs, limited data center capacity, AI monetization timelines, and competition in cloud services. 
The overall outlook remains positive, supported by contracted backlog and growing enterprise AI adoption.
